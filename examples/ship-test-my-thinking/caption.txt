Write your rough version first. Then hand it to AI and ask it to find the holes.

If AI writes your startup strategy for you, you end up in the pitch defending thinking that isn't yours. Write it first, even badly. Now AI is a sparring partner, not a ghostwriter.

Three questions founders can ask it:
Where is this weakest?
What am I assuming without evidence?
What would a skeptical investor ask first?

Fix what it finds. Ask again. You still make the call.

Save this for the next time you open a blank chat.
